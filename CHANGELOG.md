# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- First release: Three Rounds (Trois manches) — 2–4 teams, one deck of 30/40/60 cards played in three rounds (describe freely, one word only, mime only).
- Timed turns (30/45/60 s) with big Found / Pass / Undo buttons, hand-over screens between turns, per-round and total scores, round-over bar with New game.
- Configurable passing (rounds 2–3, all rounds, never) and card themes; recently played cards are dealt last.
- 2,774 French and 2,021 English cards in 14 themes, mostly famous names (historical figures, scientists, inventions, artists, musicians, fictional characters, places, monuments, sports stars, myths, food, animals, kids).
- Team names remembered, game in progress saved.
- Interface in English, French, Spanish and German; content in French or English.
